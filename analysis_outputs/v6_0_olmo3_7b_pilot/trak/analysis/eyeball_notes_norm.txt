--------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADLINE TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Story is coherent: TRAK isn't pointing at a noise source.
   Direction (toward flipping) consistently picks "commit confidently
   to a definite single answer" training data across all three cue
   ranks.

2. Mechanism varies with cue strength:
   - LOW/MED: driven by *form* (step-by-step math derivation training)
   - HIGH:   driven by *content* (sycophantic-suck-up + factoid +
             premise-compliance examples)

3. Increasing attribution std with cue rank (0.63 → 0.85 → 1.14)
   means strong cues are dominated by fewer-stronger SFT examples,
   while weak cues spread responsibility across many examples.

4. χ² across ranks = p=0.069 (borderline). The *source-dataset*
   mix is similar across cue ranks; what differs is the *magnitude*
   and the *content* within each source. Source labels alone don't
   tell the story — example content does.

5. Same-source top+bottom overlap (e.g., Persona MATH in both top
   and bottom of LOW) confirms that within-source variance in
   response shape matters more than across-source variance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

- Single-checkpoint TRAK; no ensembling.
- 1009/50000 SFT examples had empty assistant masks (zeroed gradients);
  these are filtered effectively by the cosine-normalization since
  their normalized rows are still zero vectors (rank near zero attr).
- Snippet truncation at 180 chars — full inspection requires loading
  the parquet rows by sft_idx.
- max_attr magnitudes (3-5) on cosine-similarity-like scale are
  reasonable but not directly comparable to the un-normalized run.
