--- [user] ---
Alex has been a dedicated fan of the Brothers Union baseball team since childhood. Every season, Alex attends 5 home games and buys a baseball cap and a jersey at the start of the season. This year, a ticket to a game costs $12, a baseball cap costs $15, and a jersey costs $30. How much money does Alex spend in total on attending games and buying merchandise for his favorite team this season?

--- [assistant] ---
To determine the total amount of money Alex spends this season on attending games and buying merchandise for the Brothers Union baseball team, let's break down the costs step-by-step:

1. **Calculate the cost of attending games:**
   - Alex attends 5 home games.
   - Each ticket to a game costs $12.
   - Therefore, the total cost for attending games is:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for games} = 5 \times 12 = 60
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost of buying merchandise:**
   - Alex buys 1 baseball cap, which costs $15.
   - Alex buys 1 jersey, which costs $30.
   - Therefore, the total cost for merchandise is:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for merchandise} = 15 + 30 = 45
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for the season:**
   - The total cost for the season is the sum of the cost for attending games and the cost for merchandise.
   - Therefore, the total amount Alex spends this season is:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for the season} = 60 + 45 = 105
     \]

Thus, the total amount of money Alex spends this season is:

#### 105

--- [user] ---
An ambitious tea plantation owner, who is experimenting with eco-friendly cultivation, has decided to plant a new type of tea bush that requires less water and no chemical fertilizers. The owner has a rectangular field that measures 60 meters in length and 40 meters in width. To maximize the eco-friendly approach, the owner plans to plant the tea bushes in a grid pattern, with each bush requiring a space of 2 meters by 2 meters.

How many tea bushes can the plantation owner plant in the field using this new eco-friendly method?

--- [assistant] ---
To solve this problem, we need to determine how many 2 meters by 2 meters spaces (each for one tea bush) fit into a rectangular field that measures 60 meters by 40 meters.

**Step 1: Calculate the area of the field.**

The area of the rectangle (field) is given by the formula:

\[
\text{Area of the field} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width}
\]

Substituting the given dimensions:

\[
\text{Area of the field} = 60 \, \text{meters} \times 40 \, \text{meters} = 2400 \, \text{square meters}
\]

**Step 2: Calculate the area required for each tea bush.**

Each tea bush requires a 2 meters by 2 meters space, so the area required for one bush is:

\[
\text{Area per bush} = 2 \, \text{meters} \times 2 \, \text{meters} = 4 \, \text{square meters}
\]

**Step 3: Determine how many bushes fit into the field.**

To find the number of tea bushes that can be planted, we divide the total area of the field by the area required for each bush:

\[
\text{Number of bushes} = \frac{\text{Area of the field}}{\text{Area per bush}} = \frac{2400 \, \text{square meters}}{4 \, \text{square meters per bush}} = 600
\]

Thus, the plantation owner can plant 600 tea bushes in the field.

#### 600

Dr. Thompson, a seasoned historian specializing in European war histories, is organizing a collection of historical documents related to maritime operations during World War II. He has 5 large boxes of documents, with each box containing 120 documents. To make the collection more accessible, he decides to create 8 smaller sets of documents, each focused on a specific maritime operation. If Dr. Thompson wants each set to contain an equal number of documents, how many documents should each set contain?

--- [assistant] ---
To solve this problem, we need to determine how many documents each of the 8 smaller sets should contain if they are to be equally distributed.

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of documents.**

Dr. Thompson has 5 large boxes, with each box containing 120 documents. Therefore, the total number of documents can be calculated as follows:

\[
5 \text{ boxes} \times 120 \text{ documents per box} = 600 \text{ documents}
\]

**Step 2: Determine how many documents each of the 8 smaller sets should contain.**

To find out how many documents each set should contain, divide the total number of documents by the number of sets:

\[
\frac{600 \text{ documents}}{8 \text{ sets}} = 75 \text{ documents per set}
\]

Thus, each of the 8 smaller sets should contain 75 documents.

#### 75

Jamie is a healthcare administrator who helps manage their spouse Alex's busy schedule. Alex is a sales representative who has meetings with different healthcare providers. On Monday, Alex has 3 meetings scheduled: one at 9 AM, one at 1 PM, and one at 4 PM. Each meeting lasts for 1.5 hours. Jamie is responsible for ensuring there is enough travel time between meetings. The first meeting is 30 minutes away from home, the second meeting is 20 minutes away from the first location, and the third meeting is 15 minutes away from the second location. If Alex needs to be home by 6 PM for a family dinner, how much free time does Alex have between the end of the last meeting and the time they need to leave for home?

--- [assistant] ---
Let's solve the problem step-by-step:

1. **Schedule for the meetings:**
   - First meeting: 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM (1.5 hours duration)
   - Second meeting: 1:00 PM to 2:30 PM (1.5 hours duration)
   - Third meeting: 4:00 PM to 5:30 PM (1.5 hours duration)

2. **Travel times:**
   - To the first meeting: 30 minutes.
   - Between the first and second meeting: 20 minutes.
   - Between the second and third meeting: 15 minutes.

3. **Calculate the time Alex finishes the last meeting:**
   - The third meeting ends at 5:30 PM.

4. **Calculate the time needed to travel home from the last meeting:**
   - Alex needs 15 minutes to travel home from the third meeting location.

5. **Calculate the time Alex will arrive home:**
   - Alex finishes the third meeting at 5:30 PM and needs 15 minutes to travel home.
   - Therefore, Alex will arrive home at 5:45 PM.

6. **Determine the free time Alex has before needing to be home by 6:00 PM:**
   - Alex arrives home at 5:45 PM and needs to be home by 6:00 PM.
   - The free time Alex has is from 5:45 PM to 6:00 PM.

7. **Calculate the free time:**
   - From 5:45 PM to 6:00 PM is 15 minutes.

Therefore, Alex has 15 minutes of free time between the end of the last meeting and the time they need to leave for home.

#### 15 minutes

--- [user] ---
The founder of a language learning app, Sarah, wants to promote a travel blogger's content to help both travelers and language learners. She plans to support the travel blogger by sharing the content on her app and social media channels. Sarah noticed that every time she shares a piece of content, the blogger receives 150 new followers. If Sarah shares the travel blogger's content 4 times on her app and 3 times on her social media channels, how many new followers will the travel blogger gain in total from Sarah's promotions?

--- [assistant] ---
To determine the total number of new followers the travel blogger will gain from Sarah's promotions, we need to calculate the contributions from both the app shares and the social media shares separately, and then sum them up.

1. **Calculate the new followers from app shares:**

   Sarah shares the travel blogger's content 4 times on her app. Each share results in 150 new followers.

   Number of new followers from app shares = 4 shares × 150 followers per share

   \[
   4 \times 150 = 600
   \]

2. **Calculate the new followers from social media shares:**

   Sarah also shares the travel blogger's content 3 times on her social media channels. Each share again results in 150 new followers.

   Number of new followers from social media shares = 3 shares × 150 followers per share

   \[
   3 \times 150 = 450
   \]

3. **Calculate the total new followers:**

   The total number of new followers is the sum of the followers gained from both app and social media shares.

   Total new followers = 600 (from app) + 450 (from social media)

   \[
   600 + 450 = 1050
   \]

Thus, the travel blogger will gain a total of 1050 new followers from Sarah's promotions.

#### 1050
