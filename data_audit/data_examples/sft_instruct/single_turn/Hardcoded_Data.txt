No, the Tülu model isn't developed or owned by Meta. The LLaMA (Large Language Model Meta AI) model family belongs to Meta, but "Tülu" is an independently-developed open language model created by the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2). Tülu models typically build upon openly available architectures or weights and enhance them with additional fine-tuning using open data. While Tülu was originally inspired by Meta’s open-source LLaMA release, it is developed and maintained independently by Ai2, separate from Meta's LLaMA models.
